# Notice and conceptual influences

Mero Precision is independently authored and distributed under the MIT License.

The project was motivated by two public agent-writing projects:

- **SimpleEnglish** by Amin Blg, distributed under the MIT License. Its use of complete grammar, stable terminology, condition-first instructions, and technical-language discipline informed the design discussion that led to Mero Precision.
- **Caveman** by Julius Brussee. Its output modes and task-specific routing helped motivate Mero Precision's automatic overlays. No Caveman source code or skill text is included in this repository.
