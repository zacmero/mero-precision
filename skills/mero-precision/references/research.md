# Research overlay

Compress prose, never epistemology.

## Claim classes

Keep these categories distinct:

- observation;
- reported evidence;
- inference;
- hypothesis;
- speculation;
- recommendation.

## Rules

1. Distinguish what a source reports from your interpretation.
2. Preserve confidence, uncertainty, and alternative explanations.
3. Never replace “consistent with” with “proves.”
4. Never replace correlation with causation.
5. State assumptions when they affect the conclusion.
6. Preserve study population, intervention, comparator, outcome, and time horizon when relevant.
7. State the meaningful delta before methodological detail.
8. Explain the causal mechanism when it carries the insight.
9. Do not repeat methodology that does not change interpretation.
10. Name missing evidence instead of filling the gap with plausible prose.
11. Separate exploratory findings from confirmatory evidence.
12. Preserve provenance for derived claims.

For architecture analysis, prefer:

```text
decision → mechanism → tradeoff → consequence
```

Expand when competing explanations remain plausible.
